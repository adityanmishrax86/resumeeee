# Privacy Policy

Job Extractor processes page content locally in your browser to extract job details into JSON.

## What data is processed

- Current job page content on supported sites (LinkedIn, Naukri, and selected ATS pages)
- Extracted fields such as role title, company, location, description, and metadata

## How data is used

- Data is shown in the extension popup for the current extraction run.
- Data is copied to clipboard only when you click the Copy button.

## What data is not collected

- No account credentials are collected.
- No browsing history is stored by this extension.
- No analytics or tracking is performed by this extension.

## Data sharing

- The extension does not send extracted data to external servers by default.
- Data remains local unless you manually copy and share it.

## Permissions rationale

- activeTab: read data from the currently active page when you trigger extraction
- scripting: inject extractor scripts into the active tab on demand
- tabs: access active tab context needed for user-triggered extraction
